Teya Weir is a sound designer and songwriter born and raised in rural Ohio. Her time in nature as a child left her with a fascination for naturally occurring patterns of sound: running water, wind, birdsong, and thunderstorms. She blends this inspiration with dense electronics and fragile folk melodies with the hope of bypassing the listener’s initial perception to resonate with something deeper and more innate. She often uses her voice as another instrument, subtly folding herself into the broad environment of sound she builds. However, when her vocals are front and center, she uses her lyricism to paint the universal truths of love, grief, and spirituality in a new light for the listener. Inspired by the work of contemporary poets, she arranges the words and concepts we’ve heard hundreds of times into a new order of bittersweet contrast. The overall experience of her music is dreamlike - equally meaningful and absurd, consequential and ephemeral, vivid and subliminal.
